# Privacy Notice

**Last updated: 6 November 2026**

This notice explains what personal data MageWorks collects when you visit mageworks.ai, message us on WhatsApp, fill in our enquiry form or email us; why we collect it; who handles it for us; and the choices and rights you have. It is published in English and Bahasa Malaysia. If there is any difference between the two versions, the English version prevails.

## The short version

- **Our WhatsApp replies may be written by AI.** Our Enquiry Assistant is an AI assistant. It answers first, and Chee Loung, our founder, personally follows up.
- We collect what you send us (such as your phone number, WhatsApp profile name, name, company and what you need help with) plus basic technical data.
- We use it to answer your enquiry, follow up and arrange a call. We never sell it, and we don't send marketing messages unless you've said yes.
- A few trusted providers handle it for us: Meta (WhatsApp), Anthropic (AI replies), Google (our enquiry log), Cal.com (booking calls) and our hosting provider. Some of them are outside Malaysia.
- You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time. To delete it, message **"delete my data"** on WhatsApp or email **info@mageworks.ai**. [See how](#delete-your-data).

## 3. Talking to our AI Enquiry Assistant

When you message MageWorks on WhatsApp, **the first replies you receive may be generated by AI, not typed by a person.** Here's how it works:

1. Your message reaches us through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform.
2. Our system, run on a cloud hosting provider, sends the conversation to Anthropic, whose AI model writes a reply. The reply is sent back to you on WhatsApp.
3. The assistant may ask a few simple questions (such as your name, company, industry and what you need help with) and save the key details in our enquiry log in Google Sheets.
4. If it makes sense, the assistant can offer you a time to talk, booked through Cal.com.
5. **Chee Loung reads every enquiry and follows up personally**, usually within one working day during our working hours (Monday to Friday, Malaysia business hours).

Please keep in mind:

- AI can make mistakes. Nothing the assistant says is a binding quote, offer or advice. A person will confirm anything important.
- You can ask for a person at any time, for example by typing "human".
- The assistant does not make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects. It just helps us answer and organise enquiries.
- Messages to a business on the WhatsApp Business Platform are encrypted in transit, but they are not end-to-end encrypted in the same way as chats between friends: Meta's platform decrypts them so our assistant can read and reply to them.
- Please don't share sensitive information (see section 4).

## 4. What we collect and how

**When you message us on WhatsApp**, we receive:

- your phone number and WhatsApp profile name;
- the content of your messages (text, and any files, images or voice notes you send);
- the time of each message and its delivery status; and
- anything you choose to share during the conversation, such as your name, company, industry and what you need help with.

**When you fill in our enquiry form**, we collect your name, business name, email address and your answer to "What's eating your week?". Your name, email address and message are required: without them we can't reply. Your business name is optional.

**When you email us**, we receive your email address, your name, anything in your email signature and the content of your email and attachments.

**When you book a call** through Cal.com, we collect your name, email address, the time you choose and any notes you add.

**When you visit our website**, our website host, Cloudflare (see section 12), automatically receives technical data such as your IP address, browser and device type, the page you requested and the time of your visit. We do not use this to identify you.

**Where your data comes from.** Almost always directly from you. If someone recommends us to you and passes on your contact details, we'll tell you who referred you when we first get in touch.

**Please don't send us sensitive data.** There's no need to share health information, religious or political beliefs, biometric data, financial account details or similar information in an enquiry. If you send it anyway, we'll only use it to reply to you and will delete what we don't need.

**Is it required?** Giving us your data is voluntary. If you don't give us a way to contact you, or enough detail about your enquiry, we may not be able to help.

## 7. International transfers

We're based in Malaysia, but some of our providers process data in other countries, including the United States. When your data is transferred outside Malaysia, we do so only on a basis allowed by section 129 of the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (as amended in 2024). In practice, this means the transfer is needed to answer your enquiry or take steps you asked for before a contract, or we have taken reasonable precautions and exercised due diligence, for example by using providers bound by data processing terms that protect your data.

**If you are in the EU, EEA or UK:** when you contact us, your data is sent to us in Malaysia. Malaysia does not have an "adequacy decision" from the European Commission. Where our providers transfer data onward, we rely on their safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses or, for some US providers, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Contact us if you'd like more information about these safeguards.

## 8. How long we keep it

We keep personal data only as long as we need it:

- **Enquiries and leads with no further engagement** (WhatsApp, form or email): deleted 12 months after our last contact with you.
- **Enquiry Assistant conversation records on our own system:** deleted after 90 days. The key details are kept in our enquiry log as above.
- **Copies held by our providers:** Meta keeps Cloud API messages for at most 30 days, and Anthropic deletes API inputs and outputs within 30 days by default (see section 6).
- **Cal.com bookings:** deleted 12 months after the call, unless you become a client.
- **Clients:** for the duration of our work together, then for up to 7 years to meet tax and accounting record-keeping requirements.
- **Marketing list:** until you unsubscribe. We keep a minimal record that you unsubscribed so we don't contact you again.
- **Website technical data:** as long as our hosting provider and Google keep it under their own policies.

## 9. Keeping your data safe

We take reasonable practical, technical and organisational steps to protect your data, including two-factor sign-in on our accounts, limiting access to the people who need it, encrypted connections between our systems, and using established service providers. No system is perfectly secure, but if a data breach happens that is likely to cause you significant harm, we will notify the Personal Data Protection Commissioner and you, as the law requires.

## 10. Your rights

**Under Malaysian law**, you have the right to:

- **access** the personal data we hold about you, and get a copy;
- **correct** data that is inaccurate, incomplete, misleading or out of date;
- **withdraw your consent** to our processing of your data, which includes asking us to delete it (see section 11);
- **stop direct marketing** at any time;
- **ask us to stop processing** that is likely to cause you substantial damage or distress; and
- **ask us to transmit your data to another organisation** (data portability), where this is technically feasible and the data formats are compatible.

**If the GDPR applies to you** (for example, if you are in the EU), you also have the right to have your data erased, to restrict how we use it, to object to processing based on our legitimate interests, and to receive your data in a portable format. You can also complain to your local data protection authority (in Belgium, the [Data Protection Authority](https://www.dataprotectionauthority.be)).

**How to use your rights.** Email info@mageworks.ai with the subject "Privacy request", or message us on WhatsApp, and tell us what you'd like. We may need to confirm your identity first, for example by replying from the same phone number or email address. We'll respond within 21 days. Requests are usually free; if a fee is ever allowed and applies (for example, for some access requests under Malaysian law), we'll tell you before going ahead.

If you're not happy with how we've handled your data, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also contact the [Personal Data Protection Commissioner of Malaysia](https://www.pdp.gov.my).

## 11. How to delete your data

You can ask us to delete the personal data we hold about you at any time, in either of these ways:

- **On WhatsApp:** send the message **"delete my data"** to our WhatsApp business number (shown on our website once it's live).
- **By email:** write to **info@mageworks.ai** with the subject **"Delete my data"**, and include the phone number or email address you used to contact us.

**What happens next:**

1. We confirm we've received your request within 3 working days. If we can't tell that the request comes from you, we may ask you to confirm it from the same phone number or email address.
2. Within 30 days, we delete your details from our enquiry log, our Enquiry Assistant conversation records, our emails, and any Cal.com bookings, and we tell you when it's done.
3. Copies held by our providers are removed under their own retention rules: Meta keeps Cloud API messages for at most 30 days, and Anthropic deletes API data within 30 days by default.

**What we may keep:** a minimal record that you asked us to delete your data and not to contact you again, and any records we must keep by law (for example, invoices if you were a client). We'll tell you if anything is kept and why.

**Please note:** deleting a chat in your own WhatsApp app does not delete our copy, so please ask us using one of the options above. Your WhatsApp account itself is handled by WhatsApp; see the [WhatsApp Privacy Policy](https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/privacy-policy) for how to manage it.

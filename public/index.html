Connections & MCP

Plugged into the tools you already use.

A digital employee is only as useful as what it can reach. We connect it to your tools, often using MCP (Model Context Protocol), an open standard for plugging AI into business systems.

Every connection comes with clear permissions: what it can see, and what it's allowed to do. Nothing more.

Example connection · for the Enquiry Assistant

Booking calendar

Checks free slots and books appointments straight into your calendar.

  • Can see when you're free or busy
  • Can create new bookings
  • Can't move or delete existing events
Example connection · for the Invoice Clerk

Accounting software

Turns supplier invoices into draft bills, ready for your review.

  • Can look up suppliers
  • Can create draft bills
  • Can't approve or pay anything

Inventory stock spreadsheet

  • Can't change any numbers

FAQ

Questions we hear a lot.

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly. There's no such thing as a silly AI question.

Do I need to be technical, or have an IT team?

Not at all. You know your business; we handle the tech. We explain everything in plain language, and if your team will use what we build, we'll show them how.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope, but we start small on purpose: one workflow, built and proven, before anything bigger. You'll get a clear timeline before any work starts.

What if you tell me I don't need AI?

Then that's the answer, and you've just saved money. You'll still leave with a clear picture of where a simple automation, or a small change in how work flows, could help.

Is my business data safe?

On a project, we only access what the job needs, keep your data in your own tools wherever possible, and agree in writing how it's handled. We never sell it or reuse it for anything else. When you first get in touch, a few trusted providers help us reply. Our Privacy Notice lists them all.

Will a digital employee replace my staff?

No. A digital employee takes the repetitive work off your team so they can focus on the work that needs a person. And it always reports to someone on your team.

What tools do you work with?

Usually the ones you already use: WhatsApp, Gmail or Outlook, Google Workspace or Microsoft 365, spreadsheets and common accounting software. If something can't be connected, we'll tell you upfront.

Do you work with businesses outside Malaysia?

Yes. We're based in Malaysia and work with businesses anywhere. Most of the work happens remotely.
